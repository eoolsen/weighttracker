## Description

weighttracker.io is a simple, private weight tracking app. Log your daily weight, monitor your BMI, and stay on track toward your goal — all without accounts, ads, or your data leaving your device.

**Features:**

- Log your weight with a single tap
- View your weight trend over time in a clean line chart
- Automatic BMI calculation — just enter your height once
- Goal Progress tracker showing how far you've come and how close you are
- Edit or delete any entry at any time
- 100% private — all data stored on-device with no syncing, no accounts, no tracking

Whether you're losing weight, gaining muscle, or just staying consistent, weighttracker.io gives you the numbers without the noise.
