Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

No data collection

weighttracker.io does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter — weight entries, height, and goal weight — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework and never leaves your device.

No third-party services

The app does not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or any third-party SDKs. No data is sent to any server.

No account required

weighttracker.io requires no account, login, or registration of any kind.
